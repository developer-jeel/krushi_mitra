Frequently Asked Questions
How do I renew my premium membership?▼
Click the Renew Plan button above, or go to the Premium page. Your plan renews automatically if auto-renew is enabled. We'll remind you 7 days before expiry.
How do I cancel my membership?▼
Click Cancel Membership above or contact support. You'll keep premium access until the end of your current billing cycle.
Can I upgrade my plan anytime?▼
Yes! Upgrade from Standard to Premium anytime. The unused portion of your current plan is pro-rated and credited to the new plan.
Is a refund available?▼
First-time yearly subscribers get a 7-day money-back guarantee. Monthly plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled at any time.
Can I switch from monthly to yearly billing?▼
Absolutely. Click Change Billing above or contact support. Switching to yearly saves up to 20% on the total annual cost.
