PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides direct income support of ₹6,000/year to small and marginal farmer families across India through direct bank transfer.

💳 ₹6,000/year in 3 instalments of ₹2,000 directly to bank account
🏛 Ministry of Agriculture Details
🌾
Active
PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Comprehensive crop insurance scheme providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage due to unforeseen events like flood, drought, or pest attack.

🛡️ Premium as low as 1.5% (Rabi) & 2% (Kharif) of sum insured amount
🏛 Ministry of Agriculture Details
💳
Active
Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Provides farmers timely access to credit for agricultural needs including crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, maintenance of farm assets and allied activities.

📈 Credit up to ₹3 lakh at 4% interest p.a. with interest subvention
🏛 Ministry of Finance Details
☀️
Active
PM KUSUM Yojana

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthan Mahabhiyan — provides solar pumps and grid-connected solar power plants to farmers for sustainable irrigation.

⚡ 60% subsidy on solar pump installation; extra income by selling surplus power to grid
🏛 Ministry of New & Renewable Energy Details
🌱
Active
Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides a soil health card to every farmer containing crop-wise recommendations of nutrients and fertilizers required for individual farms to help improve productivity.

📋 Free soil testing and personalised fertiliser recommendations every 2 years
🏛 Department of Agriculture Details
📊
Expanding
eNAM — National Agriculture Market

Pan-India electronic trading portal linking existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities and ensure better price discovery.

💹 Transparent price discovery, online payment & seamless commodity movement across states
